==[ 2026-08-30 ]

bootin'

tags: presentation

hi! I'm very excited to start posting about my projects and ideas here, the goal of this site is in part forcing myself to finish my ideas, or at least bring them to a level where a person that doesn't live inside my head can understand them, and hopefully you can do something useful with what I present.

I'm slowly gathering the courage and equipment (the equipment is the least important of the two) to show off what I do on youtube, but given this month I've got a lot to do, I think that will have to wait until next month. I do have the editing experience and I know how much extra effort it is to document one's projects, specially to make it in a way that's entertaining. Thankfully at the current moment that's not something I'm particularly worried about, and this blog provides me with a middle ground I'm more than comfortable with.

I've worked in pretty diverse fields across the years, and built a lot of projects I'm really proud of. Having a log of the ideas that run through my head while working on them, my reflections, and thoughts of the process now seems like something very important to me. I think, even though the act of building a project by itself is a big amount of effort, adding this small layer of complexity might even be a little bit therapeutic, and apart from that, help me work and understand my mistakes in a healthier way.
